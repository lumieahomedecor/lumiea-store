NEED TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

How does checkout work?

Add your favorites to your bag and continue to checkout. You'll be sent to a secure hosted payment page for the available payment method.

How long is shipping?

Delivery time depends on the product and destination. Your final shipping estimate should be confirmed before purchase.

Do you ship internationally?

International delivery may be available for selected products. Availability and shipping cost depend on destination.

What if my order arrives damaged?

Contact LUMIÉA as soon as possible with your order number and clear photos/video of the package and item. We'll review the issue and help with the next steps.

Are electrical products safe?

Always check the product's voltage, plug type, installation requirements and supplier safety information for your destination before purchase.
